### 2. Description of the Service
Pocket Advisor is a personal spending, budget, and expense-management application designed to help users:
* record and organize expenses and income across multiple currencies;
* automatically identify certain transactions from bank and payment SMS messages;
* schedule and track bill, subscription, and loan EMI payment reminders;
* manage monthly budgets, shopping lists, and category spending limits;
* analyze spending patterns, burn-rate projections, and trends;
* perform personal finance calculations using built-in calculators (such as EMI, SIP, FD/RD, Savings Goal, Compound Interest, and Inflation calculators);
* manage shared group expenses and balances between users;
* calculate suggested debt settlements using a debt-minimization algorithm and generate standard UPI payment links or QR codes for settlement convenience; and
* receive AI-generated spending information and insights through the AI Copilot.

The specific features available to you may depend on your account type, device, operating system, subscription status, region, and future changes to the App.
